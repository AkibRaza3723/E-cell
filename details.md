## 1. How & Where to Send the Data

Please **do not send loose photos or random messages on WhatsApp**, as photos get compressed and text gets lost.

### ✅ Recommended Submission Method: One Master Google Drive Folder
1. Create one main Google Drive folder named:  
   👉 `E-CELL_WEBSITE_DATA_2026`
2. Inside that folder, create these sub-folders:
   - `📁 01_Home_Page_Info/` (write-ups, vision, stats, sponsor logos)
   - `📁 02_Team_Members/` (Google Sheet of members + high-res member photos)
   - `📁 03_Events/` (event posters, descriptions, registration links)
   - `📁 04_Startups/` (startup logos, founder info, write-ups)
   - `📁 05_Blogs_and_Articles/` (Google Docs of articles + cover photos)
   - `📁 06_Official_Assets/` (society logo in PNG, brochures, partner logos)
3. **Important Link Setting:**  
   Click **Share** on the Google Drive folder and set general access to **"Anyone with the link can view"** (or Editor), then share the link with the tech team.

### 📸 Photo & File Quality Rules:
- **Photos:** Always send original photos directly uploaded from your phone/camera to Google Drive. Do not use screenshots or WhatsApp forwarded images (they look blurry on laptops).
- **Text:** Write text in **Google Docs** or **Google Sheets** so the tech team can easily copy and paste without spelling mistakes.

## 2. What We Need to Complete the Home Page

Right now, the home page only has the top banner (Hero Section) and the bottom footer. To turn it into an engaging, complete landing page, we need content for the following sections:

### A. About E-Cell (Who We Are)
* **Short Introduction (3–4 sentences):** What is E-Cell? Who are we empowering? (e.g., *"E-Cell is the official entrepreneurship body of Jamia Millia Islamia dedicated to cultivating an entrepreneurial mindset..."*)
* **Vision Statement (1–2 sentences):** Where do we want our campus startups and students to reach in the future?
* **Mission Statement (2–3 bullet points):** What do we actually do on a day-to-day basis? (e.g., workshops, funding support, incubation, networking).

### B. Numbers & Impact Counter (Stats)
People love seeing numbers! Please provide 4 key numbers/milestones we can highlight on the front page:
* Number of **Active Community Members / Students Reached** (e.g., `5,000+ Students`)
* Number of **Startups Incubated / Supported** (e.g., `25+ Startups`)
* Number of **Events / Workshops Organized** (e.g., `40+ Flagship Events`)
* Total **Grants / Funding / Prize Pool Disbursed** (e.g., `₹10 Lakhs+ Prize Pool` or `₹50 Lakhs+ Funding Facilitated`)

### C. Core Pillars / "What We Offer"
Explain what a student gets when they connect with E-Cell. Provide a short 2-sentence description for 4 core pillars:
1. **Incubation & Mentorship:** How we guide ideas into registered startups.
2. **Events & Hackathons:** Our case competitions, annual summits, and speaker sessions.
3. **Investor & Network Access:** Connecting founders with venture capitalists and alumni.
4. **Skill Development:** Hands-on bootcamps in tech, marketing, finance, and pitch deck building.

### D. Featured Star Startups (Top 3)
* Which **3 best student startups** should be showcased right on the front page as our prime success stories?

### E. Past Prominent Guests, Speakers & Mentors
* Names, company names, their designation, and their photo (e.g., founders, angel investors, CEOs who spoke at our previous events).

### F. Sponsors, Institutional Partners & CIE Jamia Association
* High-resolution logos of:
  - Centre for Innovation and Entrepreneurship (CIE)
  - College / University official crest/logo
  - Past corporate sponsors, media partners, or student community partners.

### G. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
Provide 4 to 6 common questions that new students or aspiring founders ask, along with simple answers:
* *Example Q1:* Who can join E-Cell? Is it open to all departments or only B.Tech?
* *Example Q2:* How can I register my startup or idea with E-Cell?
* *Example Q3:* Are events free of cost for college students?
* *Example Q4:* When does annual core team recruitment open?

## 4. Events Details (Upcoming & Past)

For each event (upcoming workshops, hackathons, or past summits), please provide the following in a **Google Doc**:

1. **Event Title:** Clear, catchy name of the event (e.g., *E-Summit '26*, *Venture Pitch Hackathon*).
2. **Short Catchphrase / One-Liner:** A quick 1-sentence hook (e.g., *"Pitch your revolutionary startup idea to angel investors and win up to ₹1,00,000."*).
3. **Full Description:** 2 to 3 paragraphs detailing:
   - What the event is about.
   - Who should participate (eligibility).
   - What happens during the event (round details, timeline).
   - Prizes, perks, and certificates.
4. **Category / Type:** e.g., *Hackathon, Workshop, Speaker Session, Case Study, Pitch Competition, Panel Discussion*.
5. **Date & Time:**
   - Exact Date (e.g., *15 March 2026*)
   - Day of week (e.g., *Saturday*)
   - Time window (e.g., *10:00 AM – 4:00 PM IST*)
6. **Venue / Location:**
   - If offline: Hall name / Auditorium / Campus building (e.g., *CIE Auditorium, Jamia Millia Islamia*).
   - If online: Platform name (e.g., *Google Meet / Zoom*).
7. **Registration Link:**
   - Google Form link, Unstop link, or website registration page.
8. **Event Status:**
   - `Upcoming` (registrations currently open)
   - `Ongoing` (event is happening right now)
   - `Completed` (past event for archiving)
9. **Event Posters & Banners:**
   - **Vertical Poster:** Standard Instagram/Noticeboard ratio (e.g., 4:5 or 9:16).
   - **Horizontal Banner:** Landscape ratio for website cards (e.g., 16:9).
10. **Guest / Speaker Details (if applicable):**
    - Name, Title, Company/Organization, and a clear photo of the speaker.

## 5. Startups / Student Ventures Details

For startups incubated or supported by E-Cell, please provide:

1. **Startup Name:** (e.g., *Nexora Health*).
2. **High-Resolution Logo:** PNG format with a transparent background preferred.
3. **One-Line Elevator Pitch (10–15 words):** Simple explanation of what it does (e.g., *"AI-powered diagnostics platform designed for affordable rural clinics."*).
4. **Full Description (1–2 paragraphs):**
   - The problem they are solving.
   - How their product or service works.
   - Any notable traction, awards, or customer milestones.
5. **Sector / Industry:** (e.g., *HealthTech, EdTech, FinTech, AI / SaaS, CleanTech, E-Commerce*).
6. **Founders' Names & Batch:**
   - Founder 1 Name, College Department, Year, and LinkedIn profile.
   - Co-founder(s) Name, Department, Year, and LinkedIn profile.
7. **Current Stage:**
   - `Ideation` (working on prototype)
   - `MVP / Prototype` (working demo ready)
   - `Incubated at CIE` (official campus incubated)
   - `Funded / Revenue Generating` (has raised capital or active sales)
8. **Website & Social Links:**
   - Official Website URL (if available)
   - Instagram / LinkedIn / Twitter handle
   - App Store / Play Store link (if app based)

## 6. Blogs & Articles Details

For founder stories, case studies, startup guides, and editorial insights:

1. **Blog Title:** Catchy and readable title (e.g., *"How a Jamia Student Built a 10 Lakh ARR Startup from Their Hostel Room"*).
2. **Short Excerpt / Preview (2–3 sentences):** A quick summary that makes the reader want to click and read.
3. **Full Article Content:**
   - Submit as a well-spaced Google Doc.
   - Break it into subheadings, bullet points, and short readable paragraphs.
4. **Cover Image:**
   - High-quality landscape photo (16:9 ratio, width at least 1920px).
   - Related to the article topic (e.g., startup pitching, technology, team brainstorming).
5. **Author Details:**
   - Author's Full Name (e.g., *Ayesha Khan* or *E-Cell Editorial Desk*).
   - Author's Designation (e.g., *Head of Content / Student Entrepreneur*).
   - Author's Headshot / Avatar photo.
6. **Tags / Topics:** 2 to 4 keywords (e.g., `#StartupStories`, `#Funding`, `#AI`, `#StudentFounder`).
7. **Date of Article:** Date you want displayed on the blog.
